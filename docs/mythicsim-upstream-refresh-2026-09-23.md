## Why Recklessness is disabled

This is an inherited rotation default, not missing engine support. The original
[wowsims preset commit](https://github.com/ElliotWood/Forever/commit/727c4f1e18b9aa3ca7a5c0c62fd8e7cd93e28945)
introduced separate With Reck and No Reck APLs on 2025-03-11 and put No Reck first.
`ui/warrior/presets.ts` still takes that first APL as the default.
MythicSim's `scripts/build-forever-gear.mjs` explicitly copies `warrior/dps_no_reck` into
`worker/forever/presets/warrior.apl.json`. The worker applies that rotation to imports
as well as reference builds; importing talents does not choose the With Reck preset.

No Reck contains a false condition for spell 1719 and hides the last-15-seconds action.
The false action also removes Recklessness from automatic cooldown handling. With Reck
reverses those hide flags, enabling its final-15-seconds action. Updating the engine
alone does not change either preset or MythicSim's selection.

The current generated client table still gives Recklessness a 1,800-second cooldown,
15-second duration, +100 percentage points of melee critical strike chance and +20%
damage taken. A previous comment claiming 5 minutes / 12 seconds / 50% was explicitly
removed by upstream commit `8c8f68c66da0ddad278dc8fcca4778a027b533c8` as a stale
Season of Discovery comment, not a Forever mechanic.

The original preset commit does not document why No Reck was chosen. Treating a
30-minute cooldown as optional is a plausible rationale, not confirmed author intent.

### Controlled comparison on the refreshed engine

Same warrior reference gear/talents, 120 seconds, no duration variation, random seed
12345, 3,000 iterations. Only the APL changed:

| Preset | Mean DPS | Reck casts | Mean casts per fight |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| No Reck | 851.50 | 0 | 0 |
| With Reck | 882.54 | 3,000 | 1 |

With Reck was 3.65% higher for this reference build. This is not a universal
warrior gain or the reporter's character. The engine update and this audit do not
change the production pin, publish new rankings, or silently enable Recklessness.
A separate product change should expose the cooldown choice and state which setting
public rankings use.
